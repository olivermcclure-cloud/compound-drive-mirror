Compound Drive mirror

Compound Drive mirror is a personal tool. Once a day it makes a read-only copy of its owner's own Google Docs, Sheets and uploaded files on the owner's own computer, so that a loss in Google can be undone from a copy kept somewhere else.

It is used only by the person who wrote it. It isn't offered to anyone else.

It never edits, moves or deletes anything in Google Drive.
