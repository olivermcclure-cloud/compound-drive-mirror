Privacy policy

Compound Drive mirror. Last updated 19 November 2026.

What the app reads

With the read-only Google Drive permission its owner grants, the app lists the files in the owner's Drive and downloads copies of the files the owner owns. It reads nothing else from the Google account, and it never changes anything in Drive.

Where the data goes

The copies are written only to the owner's own computer. The one thing sent anywhere else is a short message to the owner's own Telegram chat when a copy fails or a file is copied only in part, which may name the file concerned. Nothing from Google is shared with any other person or company, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model. The pages on this site use no analytics and no cookies.

Keeping and deleting

The copies stay on the owner's computer, with earlier versions kept for 12 months. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the app reading Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions go to the support email address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
